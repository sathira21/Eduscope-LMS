# Eduscope LMS User Guide

Welcome to Eduscope LMS! This guide will help you navigate through the platform and make the most out of its features.

## 🎓 For Students

### 1. Getting Started
- **Login**: Use your email and password to access your dashboard.
- **Profile**: Update your personal information and profile picture.

### 2. Taking Quizzes
- **Browsing**: Explore quizzes by subject or module.
- **Starting a Quiz**: Click "Start Quiz" and read the instructions.
